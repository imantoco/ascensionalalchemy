How the Ex Detox works in practice

It's a guided meditation. You don't study anything, answer anything or write anything down in the middle. You just listen.

1

It relaxes your whole body

Your body lets go of the tension and the need for control while you simply follow along and relax. This is where a racing mind starts to slow down.

2

It goes to what matters

The longing to be with him, the sadness, the neediness and the feeling that you were abandoned. With every breath, they lose their grip.

3

It brings you back

When the meditation ends, you open your eyes and can get on with your day or go straight to sleep. More present, with a calmer mind.

4

You repeat it for 7 days

It's the same audio every day, on purpose. The repetition is what does the work. And each night you answer 4 questions in the Daily Journal.

Frequently asked questions

I've tried everything and nothing worked. Why would this be different?

Because almost everything you tried changed your schedule. Distracting yourself, working more, going out, traveling: it fills your time and leaves untouched what keeps running in your head. The Kit's practice does the opposite: about fifteen minutes in which you stop and work on exactly what you spend the day trying not to think about. And if it still isn't for you, there's the 7-day guarantee.

How long until I stop thinking about him?

No one can honestly promise you that, and be wary of anyone who does. What the Kit defines is what you do: about fifteen minutes a day, for seven days. Some women feel a difference in the first few days, others keep repeating the practice for longer. That's why there's the Daily Journal, so you can see what changed instead of depending on how you woke up today.

We never even officially dated. Is this for me?

Yes. How long it lasted matters less than how much you built up in your head. It's common for a woman who lived an undefined relationship to stay stuck longer than someone who really dated, because there was no clear ending to look back on.

Does this replace therapy?

No. The Kit is a self-awareness practice to do on your own, at home. If you're already in therapy, it goes alongside it. If you're not, it's no reason to stop looking for help.

How much time does it take each day?

About fifteen minutes of guided practice. On day zero you'll spend another fifteen minutes or so watching the preparation. After that it's just the practice, ideally before bed.
